網頁裡的法鬥A dog that actually
lives on your pages

牠不是浮在角落的貼圖。牠沿著你正在滑的貼文下緣走，跟著捲動，走到邊緣會轉身。會餓、會累、會討摸。 Not a sticker floating in the corner. It walks along the bottom edge of the posts you're reading, follows your scroll, and turns around at the edge. It gets hungry, tired, and wants attention.

功能

Features

牠會做什麼What it does

沿著貼文跑Runs along your posts

四段偵測找出頁面上「可以站的橫線」。認得的網站用專用規則，沒校過的網站靠通用探測，全部失敗才退回沿著視窗底部走。 Four layers of detection find the ledges on a page. Known sites use tuned selectors, unknown sites fall back to a generic probe, and only if everything fails does it walk along the bottom of the window.

真的要照顧It actually needs care

飽食、體力、親密、清潔四個數值隨真實時間變化。關掉瀏覽器兩天再開，牠會餓。餓了跑得慢、會聞地板、會跟你討東西吃。 Four stats — fed, energy, bond, clean — decay in real time. Close the browser for two days and it will be hungry when you come back. Hungry means slower, sniffing the floor, and begging.

接自己的 AI 金鑰Bring your own AI key

Anthropic、Google Gemini、任何 OpenAI 相容端點，本地模型也行。牠知道自己現在多餓多累，回話會跟著變。金鑰只存在你電腦上。 Anthropic, Google Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — local models included. It knows how hungry and tired it is, and answers accordingly. The key never leaves your machine.

會自己找事做It entertains itself

偶爾叼一顆球出來丟到遠處，然後衝過去撿。撿到會叫一聲、噴愛心。也會咬繩結、推 Kong、從卡片邊緣探頭偷看你。 Every so often it drops a ball across the page and sprints after it. Catching it earns a bark and a burst of hearts. It also tugs rope, pushes a Kong, and peeks at you over the edge of a card.

會髒，要洗澡It gets dirty

玩久了清潔度會掉。洗完會甩水、伸懶腰。每個照顧動作都有對應的動畫，不是只有數字在跳。 Play long enough and cleanliness drops. After a bath it shakes off the water and stretches. Every care action has its own animation, not just a number ticking up.

不會吵到你It knows when to hide

你在打字、頁面跳出視窗、影片全螢幕的時候牠會自動淡出。任何單一網站都可以隨時關掉。系統的「減少動態效果」也會照做。 It fades out while you type, when a dialog opens, or when a video goes fullscreen. Any single site can be switched off. It respects your reduced-motion setting too.

動作

Animations

31 種動作31 poses

站、跑、坐、趴、捲起來睡、翻肚、伸懶腰、吃飯、喝水、洗澡、甩水、咬繩、推 Kong、追球、探頭、轉身⋯⋯全部取自同一組素材，所以牠在任何動作之間都不會忽大忽小。 Stand, run, sit, lie, curl up, roll over, stretch, eat, drink, bathe, shake off, tug, nudge, chase, peek, turn — all cut from one consistent source, so it never changes size between poses.

你決定

You decide

牠住在哪裡由你決定You choose where it lives

預設只出現在 Threads、YouTube、Instagram、GitHub、Google 搜尋和 Yahoo。想讓牠跟你去每一個網頁，按一次就好——安裝時不會跳「讀取所有網站資料」的警告，因為那是選用權限。 By default it only appears on Threads, YouTube, Instagram, GitHub, Google Search and Yahoo. One click lets it follow you everywhere — and because that permission is optional, installing never triggers the scary "read all your data" warning.

  • 沒有伺服器。No server. 開發者收不到、也看不到你的任何資料。 The developer never receives or sees any of your data.
  • 不讀網頁內容。Never reads page content. 除非你主動打開「讓牠知道頁面標題」，那也只送標題的前 80 字。 Unless you turn on "let it see the page title" — and even then only the first 80 characters.
  • 信箱、雲端硬碟、登入頁、金流頁永遠排除。Mail, cloud storage, sign-in and payment pages are always excluded. 你在輸密碼的時候不該有狗在旁邊翻肚。 You shouldn't have a dog rolling over while you type a password.
  • 沒有廣告、沒有分析工具、沒有第三方程式碼。No ads, no analytics, no third-party code.
